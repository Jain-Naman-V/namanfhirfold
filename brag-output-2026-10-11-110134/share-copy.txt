Announcing NamanFHIRFOLD

Fold the structure. Keep every detail.

NamanFHIRFOLD compresses FHIR bundles up to 99% for LLM prompts — while retaining 100% of clinical facts. Output formats: compact JSON, MedPrompt text, FHIRBench markdown, CDS Hooks prefetch.
